About
Emod.

Growth executive, entrepreneur, builder. The thinking behind the work, in four parts.

How I think

Growth is a discipline. Systems beat tactics. I measure what compounds: the cost of a lead, the quality of the pipeline, the revenue the system supports. A campaign is a test. A system is the asset.

What I build

Revenue systems, intelligent operations, and enduring digital assets. The work connects demand generation, conversion, and retention around the economics of the business — then uses technology and automation to make good work repeatable.

Where I have worked

More than 15 years across technology, legal services, automotive, real estate, and commerce. From hands-on paid search to director-level leadership. From agency portfolios of 70+ accounts to building an in-house marketing team from one person to ten.

Why it matters

The point is long-term value: businesses that endure, systems that keep working, and work that compounds. That is what I build toward.
