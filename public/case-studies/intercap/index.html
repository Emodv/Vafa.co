Case study · Intercap · Jan 2023 – Mar 2025

Building an in-house marketing team from scratch.

Director of Marketing at Intercap, a Canadian venture capital firm, January 2023 to March 2025. I built the in-house marketing function that supported a selection of Intercap's portfolio companies, including the go-to-market for .inc and .box.

The outcome

  • Team: from 1 to 10+ specialists.
  • ARR supported: from $5M to $10M.
  • Lead volume: up 50%.
  • Cost per lead: from about $180 to about $70 (approximate, Emod-provided).
  • Monthly budget: $250K.

These are outcomes of the portfolio marketing system, built alongside product, sales, and the portfolio companies. ARR is portfolio revenue the system supported, not revenue one person closed alone.

My role
Director of Marketing

Scope
Portfolio marketing · .inc and .box go-to-market

Situation

Intercap is a Canadian venture capital firm. Its portfolio includes premium domains and digital products, including .inc and .box. When I joined, there was no in-house marketing function. The work had to be built, not inherited.

Mandate

Build one marketing system that could serve more than one business at once: paid acquisition, lifecycle nurture, and go-to-market, with reporting that connected spend to revenue.

The system built

  • Team. From one person to 10+ specialists across paid media, email, social, SEO, content, analytics, automation, and web development, with a set reporting cadence.
  • Paid acquisition. Google Ads, Meta, and LinkedIn, tuned by bid optimization, audience refinement, campaign structure, and funnel fixes.
  • Lifecycle and CRM. Lead nurture and segmentation, with Close CRM pipeline workflows and Twilio SMS carrying leads toward sales.
  • Outbound. More than 1M emails a month through Mailgun and SendGrid.
  • Measurement. Reporting built around the customer journey: UTM systems, Microsoft Clarity, and Mixpanel, so spend could be traced to outcomes.
  • AI-assisted operations. AI-assisted workflows raised campaign and reporting output without adding equivalent headcount.

Go-to-market: .inc and .box

Partnered with product and revenue on go-to-market for .inc and .box. Positioning and pre-launch demand produced thousands of interested prospects before launch.

Measurable outcome

ARR supported grew from $5M to $10M while the system was in place. Lead volume rose 50%. Cost per lead fell from about $180 to about $70. The team grew from 1 to 10+ specialists on a $250K monthly budget.

Lessons

  • Specialization beats headcount. A lean team where each person owns a lane scales further than a larger generalist team.
  • Attribution is the system. UTMs, Clarity, and Mixpanel turned marketing from activity into evidence.
  • Build the capability, not the campaign. The same system served multiple portfolio businesses, which is why it kept paying off.
