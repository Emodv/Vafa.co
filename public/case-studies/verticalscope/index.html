Case study · VerticalScope · 2018–2022

Affiliate growth,
held to the line.

Senior Growth Marketing Manager at VerticalScope, Toronto, 2018 to 2022. Affiliate marketing, programmatic, ecommerce, and automotive media across five high-traffic consumer properties: RateMDs, AutoGuide, RedFlagDeals, FitRated, and TreadmillReviews.net.

The outcome

  • Affiliate revenue: up 75%.
  • CPC: down 45%.
  • Black Friday program: about $1.5M, scaled only while the roughly 3:1 revenue-to-spend line held.
  • Properties: five high-traffic consumer properties.
My role
Senior Growth Marketing Manager

Scope
Paid search · Display · Native · Programmatic

Context

Five high-traffic consumer properties, each with its own audience and revenue mix. My mandate was growth across paid search, display, native, and programmatic — and I owned pacing, retargeting, and what got killed.

Challenge

Growth had to come from buying better, not buying more. On high-volume properties, traffic costs move fast. The discipline was holding the revenue line while scaling spend.

What I did

  • Algorithmic bidding and audience optimization to cut CPC 45% while affiliate revenue grew 75%.
  • Programmatic traffic arbitrage. Bought lower-cost display and native through DSPs and converted it on fitness, health, and automotive audiences.
  • Owned the kill decisions. Pacing, retargeting, and what got cut were mine to decide, and cutting early protected the line.
  • Black Friday, held to 3:1. Led a program of about $1.5M, scaling only while the roughly 3:1 revenue-to-spend line held.

Result

Affiliate revenue up 75% and CPC down 45% across the five properties. The Black Friday program ran at about $1.5M without breaking the 3:1 line.

What it taught me

  • The line is the strategy. A ratio held under pressure is worth more than a bigger budget.
  • Arbitrage is a skill. Buy attention where it is cheap, convert it where intent is real.
  • Kill decisions are as valuable as launches. What you stop funding is what keeps the program honest.

Attribution note: revenue and CPC figures are from the VerticalScope program as reported in Emod's own career record, 2018–2022.
