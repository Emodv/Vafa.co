Case study · Mirian Law Firm · 2023, returning 2026

Expensive clicks,
honest answers.

Ross Mirian's personal-injury firm in Thornhill. Google Ads and YouTube, July to October 2023. Ross returned in November 2026 for a PPC audit and a YouTube campaign.

The outcome

  • Traffic: 4,800+ users a month at a $0.89 CPC (October 2023 report).
  • Mobile: 88% of users, from Toronto, Brampton, and Mississauga.
  • The honest result: inquiries arrived, but the injuries were not severe enough to become cases. Ads paused November 1, 2023.
  • The return: Ross came back in November 2026 for a PPC audit and a YouTube campaign.
My role
Paid search & YouTube
Client
Mirian Law Firm, Thornhill
Dates
Jul – Oct 2023, returning Nov 2026
Scope
Google Ads · YouTube · PPC audit

Context

Personal-injury search is among the most expensive auctions in Canada. At agency level, Emod managed PI accounts at $50 to $200+ CPC. Mirian needed traffic that could become cases, not just clicks.

Challenge

Cheap traffic is easy. Traffic that survives intake is hard. The campaign had to find people with serious injuries in the GTA — and be honest about what it found.

What I did

  • Google Ads, July to October 2023. Core keywords to the homepage with UTM tracking, and a plan to split into geo campaigns for Oshawa, Pickering, and other GTA markets.
  • Mobile landing pages built for the campaign, and YouTube work alongside search.
  • Reported what the traffic actually did. The October 2023 report showed 4,800+ users a month at $0.89 CPC — and that the inquiries were not severe enough to become cases.
  • Paused. Ads went on hold November 1, 2023, rather than keep spending on traffic that did not clear the bar.

Result

Volume at a low cost: 4,800+ users a month at $0.89 CPC, 88% mobile. But volume was not the result. The result was that the traffic did not produce cases the firm could take — and the honest move was to pause.

What it taught me

  • A low CPC is not a result. In personal injury, the result is a case the firm can take.
  • Pausing is a decision, not a failure. Stopping spend on the wrong traffic protects the client and the relationship.
  • Returning clients are the real metric. Ross came back in November 2026 for a PPC audit and a YouTube campaign — first for the Farsi-speaking GTA community, then for the general GTA population. The firm is multilingual: Farsi, Dari, Arabic, Cantonese, and Mandarin.

Attribution note: traffic figures are from the October 2023 report. No cases signed, viewings, or revenue are claimed. The 2026 return is a PPC audit and YouTube campaign, as requested by the client in November 2026.
