How the system works

This is the system I build for a development. It describes the approach, not outcomes already achieved.

  1. Development and buyer research. Understand the project and who it is for.
  2. Market and audience selection. Choose where to run and who to reach.
  3. Project landing page and campaign. One page per project, with campaigns built around it.
  4. Lead capture and qualification workflow. Forms and WhatsApp, with a workflow to sort inquiries.
  5. Broker follow-up. Leads handed to broker partners.
  6. Measurement. Inquiries, qualified buyers, viewings, reservations, and sales tracked, so the system can be improved.

Scope note: this page describes documented project and campaign work. It is not a claim of units sold, viewings booked, or a broad record of international luxury sales.
